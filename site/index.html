How it works

Click a link. Land in the right place.

  1. 1

    Make it your default browser

    BrowserBro sits quietly in the menu bar. macOS hands it the links you click in other apps — mail, chat, notes.

  2. 2

    A rule decides

    Your rules run top to bottom; the first match wins. The link opens in that browser and profile, and a small glass Pulse at your pointer shows where it went. Click the Pulse to open it somewhere else.

  3. 3

    Or you pick, with one key

    No rule? The picker opens at your pointer, with the first browser right under it. Press 1–9, or click. Several links at once wait politely in a queue.

Rules

Rules that read like a sentence.

Match on what you know about a link — where it points, which app it came from, which key you held. Combine conditions with all or any, flip one with NOT.

Example rules cycle here, for example: when the domain is linear.app, open in Chrome, profile Work.

Build from source

Free. Build it yourself.

You need macOS 26 and Swift 6 (Xcode or the Command Line Tools). The script builds a release app and signs it ad-hoc — no paid developer account needed.

Build build/BrowserBro.app
# in the BrowserBro source folder
scripts/build-app.sh
Build and install to /Applications
scripts/build-app.sh --install
Run the tests
swift test

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is BrowserBro free?

Yes. It is free and MIT-licensed. You build it from source with one script.

Does it send my links anywhere?

No. BrowserBro makes no network calls, has no accounts and no analytics. Everything happens on your Mac.

What happens when no rule matches?

The picker opens at your mouse pointer, with the first browser right under it. Press a number, use the arrows and Return, or click. Esc cancels.

Can I edit my rules by hand?

Yes. Rules live in a human-editable file: ~/Library/Application Support/BrowserBro/rules.json.

Which browsers work with profiles?

Chrome, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi (via the profile folder); Firefox, Zen and LibreWolf (via the profile); Arc (via Spaces). Safari works as a browser.

How do I open a link in a private window?

Hold ⌥ when you choose a target in the picker.
